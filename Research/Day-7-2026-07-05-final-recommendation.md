## Position 1: NVDA — NVIDIA Corporation
**Allocation: $300 (30% of portfolio)**

### Why NVDA Gets the Largest Piece

NVIDIA is the single most important company in the AI infrastructure buildout, and after pulling back 17% from its May 2026 high of $235 to ~$195, it's trading at **21.85x forward earnings** — roughly the same multiple as the average S&P 500 stock. A company growing revenue 65% per year at market-average valuations is the definition of an opportunity.

This is the portfolio's anchor. It has real revenues ($215 billion in fiscal year 2026), real earnings, and a dominant position that would take years for any competitor to challenge. Every major tech company on Earth is a NVIDIA customer. Every dollar Microsoft, Google, Amazon, and Meta spend building AI data centers is a dollar that flows toward NVIDIA chips.

### Entry Strategy

**Buy $300 worth at current market price (~$195).** Don't try to time this one. The next major catalyst — Q2 FY2027 earnings — is expected in late August 2026. Before that, Microsoft, Amazon, Google, and Meta all report their quarterly earnings in late July. Their comments on AI infrastructure spending will move NVIDIA's stock. If you wait for a "perfect dip," you risk missing a move higher driven by those earnings reports.

The $185–$200 range has been a floor multiple times in 2026. You're buying near the bottom of recent support, not the top.

### What to Watch: Weeks 1–4

- **Late July 2026 (Big Tech earnings season):** Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta all report Q2 earnings. Listen carefully to what they say about data center spending. Any increase in AI capex guidance = good for NVDA. Any reduction or pause = sell signal.
- **August 2026 (NVDA Q2 FY2027 earnings):** The company will report whether data center revenue maintained its $75B+ quarterly pace. An earnings beat is the next major rally catalyst.

### When to Reconsider (Re-evaluation Triggers)

- A major hyperscaler (Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta) explicitly reduces AI infrastructure spending on their earnings call — consider trimming or exiting.
- US export restrictions are expanded beyond China to additional major markets (Middle East, Southeast Asia) — this would remove billions in potential revenue.
- A competitor releases a chip that meaningfully closes the performance gap at a lower price — start monitoring market share data.

### Bear Case Reminder

If AI spending at the big tech companies slows, or if NVIDIA's growth rate decelerates from 65% to 20%, the stock could compress 30-40% from current levels — even without any real damage to the business. The risk here is not that NVIDIA fails. The risk is that the stock re-rates down because investors expected even more.

## Position 2: CEG — Constellation Energy Corporation
**Allocation: $250 (25% of portfolio)**

### Why CEG Gets the Second-Largest Piece

Constellation Energy is the **most interesting risk/reward in this entire portfolio**. Here's why: the company owns the largest fleet of nuclear power plants in the United States, has signed long-term power contracts with Microsoft, Amazon, and Walmart to supply clean, always-on electricity to AI data centers, and is spending $3.9 billion to increase nuclear output. The business has never been stronger.

And yet the stock is trading at its **52-week low** — down 42% from its high of $413. That decline isn't driven by any deterioration in Constellation's business. It's investor rotation out of "AI power" plays and profit-taking after a big 2025 run. The fundamentals say one thing; the stock price says another. For a long-term investor, that gap is where opportunity lives.

The analyst consensus (18 analysts, predominantly Buy ratings, average target of $357–374) implies **50–57% upside** from the current ~$239 price. Morgan Stanley, a blue-chip Wall Street firm, has an "Overweight" rating and $364 target.

### Entry Strategy

**Buy in two tranches:**
- **Tranche 1: $175 now at ~$239.** You're buying at the 52-week low (actually slightly below it). Getting in while others are still selling.
- **Tranche 2: $75 if the stock falls to $225–230.** That zone is the next major support level. If CEG dips there before recovering, adding there would improve your average cost.

If CEG never pulls back to $225, you still own the first $175 tranche at a compelling price.

### What to Watch: Weeks 1–4

- **New power purchase agreement (PPA) announcements:** Constellation is in talks with multiple data center operators for long-term power contracts. Any new contract announcement — especially with Google, Meta, or a major data center company — would be a significant positive catalyst.
- **Regulatory approvals for nuclear uprates:** Each approval that allows a nuclear plant to increase its output is incremental revenue.

### When to Reconsider (Re-evaluation Triggers)

- A major nuclear power safety incident anywhere in the United States — regardless of whether Constellation's plants are involved. Regulatory and public backlash can hurt the entire industry.
- A key tech company (Microsoft, Amazon) publicly scales back its long-term electricity commitment or cancels a PPA.
- Electricity prices collapse due to an unexpected surge in domestic natural gas supply, reducing revenue from uncontracted nuclear output.

### Bear Case Reminder

The most honest bear case: the stock already fell 42% from its peak and could fall further before the catalyst that drives recovery materializes. Buying at the 52-week low is a fundamentally sound strategy, but "52-week low" can become "53-week low" and then "54-week low." This position requires patience — measured in months, not days.

## Position 3: KTOS — Kratos Defense & Security Solutions
**Allocation: $200 (20% of portfolio)**

### Why KTOS Gets the Third Slot

Kratos is the **best risk-adjusted opportunity in this portfolio**, which is why it gets a meaningful allocation despite being a smaller, less well-known company.

Here's the case in one paragraph: The world is in a drone warfare super-cycle. Ukraine, Taiwan, the Middle East, and now the US's own Golden Dome missile defense initiative have made it undeniable that the next generation of warfare involves cheap, expendable unmanned systems rather than $2 billion fighter jets. Kratos builds exactly those systems — tactical drones, rocket propulsion, hypersonic test vehicles. Their drone segment grew 30.9% organically in Q1 2026. Their rocket systems grew 45.8%. And yet the stock trades at only **4x trailing revenue**, which is modest for a company with these growth rates. Nine Wall Street analysts have Buy ratings on the stock; the average price target of $94–$109 implies **70-96% upside** from the current price of $55.

On July 2, 2026, Wedbush initiated coverage calling Kratos the "merchant arms supplier to the entire US defense complex" with an $85 target — a fresh endorsement from a respected analyst. Fresh analyst coverage on small-cap defense companies is a meaningful catalyst for institutional interest.

### Entry Strategy

**Buy $200 at current price (~$55).** The stock pulled back from $63 earlier in July to $55, which is itself a better entry than a week ago. The $50–52 zone is strong technical support. If the market dips further in July and you can add at $50-52, that would be ideal — but the current price is already attractive.

At $55 and six shares, the dollar amounts are small enough that you can hold through volatility without panic.

### What to Watch: Weeks 1–4

- **Congressional defense spending:** Any news about supplemental defense spending packages, especially allocations toward unmanned systems, directly benefits Kratos.
- **New contract announcements:** Kratos receives frequent contract announcements (the $36M sole-source award came on July 2). Each new contract adds to backlog visibility.
- **August 2026 earnings (Q2 FY2026):** Watch whether drone and rocket system segments maintain double-digit organic growth rates.

### When to Reconsider (Re-evaluation Triggers)

- A competitor (Shield AI, Anduril, or a traditional defense contractor) wins a major unmanned systems contract that was expected to go to Kratos.
- Congress passes a continuing resolution (CR) that freezes defense spending growth — this delays new contract awards.
- The drone or rocket systems segments report negative revenue growth — a signal that the growth story is softening.

### Bear Case Reminder

The stock was already up ~72% at its 2026 peak. Some of the easy money in the current cycle has been made. Institutional investors who bought at $30-35 may sell into rallies, creating resistance. Additionally, defense budgets are politically dependent — the annual appropriations fight in Congress can delay or reduce funding.

## Position 4: PLTR — Palantir Technologies
**Allocation: $150 (15% of portfolio)**

### Why PLTR Makes the Cut (Despite the Valuation)

Palantir gets the smallest allocation among the anchors — and for good reason. The P/E ratio (131x trailing earnings) and price-to-sales ratio (46x) are difficult to justify by any traditional metric. The stock is down 27% year-to-date despite reporting 85% revenue growth. That's the market telling you: "we know the business is great, but we think it was overpriced at the January highs."

So why include it at all?

Because the Golden Dome contract changes the math. The US government's $185 billion missile defense system — which Palantir is a core software backbone of — could deliver **$18.2 billion in government revenue to Palantir through 2028**. Their current annual run rate is about $6.5 billion. If that estimate is even half-right, Palantir's revenue doubles in two years from government contracts alone. Add in commercial AI growth (AIP — their enterprise AI platform) and a new sovereign AI partnership with NVIDIA, and you have the ingredients for a genuinely transformational few years.

At $150 (a small 15% position), the goal is exposure to that outcome without making it a portfolio-defining bet. If PLTR doubles, it adds $150 to the portfolio. If it falls 40%, it costs $60. That's the right bet size for a stock with this risk profile.

### Entry Strategy

**Buy in two tranches:**
- **Tranche 1: $75 at current price (~$130).** Get in at current levels — the NVDA/Palantir sovereign AI partnership announced July 1 is a fresh catalyst and the stock just closed up 9% on it. The momentum is positive.
- **Tranche 2: $75 held for potential pullback to $120 or below.** The stock has found support at $120 twice in 2026. If July's trading brings another market-wide dip, that's your second entry.

Do not chase PLTR if it runs above $145 before you get the second tranche in. The valuation at higher prices becomes even less defensible.

### What to Watch: Weeks 1–4

- **Golden Dome contract developments:** Any announcement of expanded task orders, new government contracts, or sovereign government AI deals would be a significant catalyst.
- **August 2026 Q2 earnings:** Full-year guidance is for ~71% revenue growth. If Q2 revenue comes in strong (continuing the 85% YoY pace from Q1), the stock should hold or rally. If growth decelerates toward 40%, get out.
- **NVIDIA/Palantir sovereign AI partnership specifics:** The deal was announced July 1 but financial details weren't disclosed. When details emerge about the commercial model, watch for whether it's truly additive or just a press release.

### When to Reconsider (Re-evaluation Triggers)

- Q2 2026 revenue growth is materially below 71% (the company's own full-year guidance implies 71%). Any slip here is a serious warning sign at this valuation.
- The Golden Dome contract scope is reduced, delayed, or its revenue timeline extends beyond 2028.
- Management indicates a slowdown in new enterprise AIP customer additions — the commercial side of the business needs to keep scaling.

### Bear Case Reminder

This is the most dangerous stock in the portfolio from a pure valuation standpoint. At 46x sales and 131x earnings, there is almost no margin for error. A disappointing quarter doesn't just cost you the quarterly upside — it can cut the stock by 40-50% because the entire valuation is based on the market's belief in perfection. Keep the $150 allocation and resist any temptation to add more unless the fundamentals are very clearly improving.

## Position 5: SMMT — Summit Therapeutics
**Allocation: $100 (10% of portfolio)**

### Why SMMT Is the Right Moonshot

Every aggressive growth portfolio should have one position that is explicitly a high-risk, high-reward bet. SMMT is that position for Freddy. It's small enough that if it goes wrong, the damage is manageable. It's large enough that if it goes right, you feel it in the portfolio.

Here's the defined event: On **November 14, 2026**, the FDA will announce whether Summit Therapeutics' cancer drug ivonescimab is approved for patients with EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer (a specific type of lung cancer). The drug attacks tumors two ways simultaneously — an approach that has shown a 34% reduction in mortality risk compared to the current standard of care.

The math:
- **If approved:** The stock was at $30.98 at its 52-week high. The current price is $15. Approval would likely push the stock back toward that level — roughly **a 100% return from $15**. Your $100 position becomes ~$200.
- **If rejected:** The stock likely falls to the $7–10 range based on biotech precedents. Your $100 position becomes $45–65 — a loss of $35–55.

That's roughly a 2-to-1 upside/downside ratio with a defined, less-than-five-months-away decision date. That's why SMMT gets the allocation despite being the most speculative pick.

### Entry Strategy

**Buy the full $100 now at ~$15.** Unlike the other positions where dollar-cost averaging makes sense, a binary event stock can move sharply at any moment before the FDA decision. A positive regulatory communication, a Big Pharma partnership announcement, or favorable trial data could send the stock up 30-50% overnight before November 14. If you're waiting to get a better price, you might miss the pre-PDUFA run.

The one exception: if the stock falls to $12–13 (near the 52-week low of $12.55), consider adding another $50 from a future cash infusion if you have it available — not from the current $1,000 budget.

### What to Watch: Weeks 1–4

- **November 14, 2026 FDA decision:** This is the one and only thing that matters for this position. Mark the date in your calendar.
- **Big Pharma partnership rumors:** A licensing deal with a major pharmaceutical company before the FDA decision would validate the drug and likely push the stock significantly higher. Watch for any M&A or partnership news involving Summit.
- **HARMONi-6 trial updates:** Additional data from their squamous NSCLC trial (a different patient population) could expand the addressable market and generate positive sentiment before November.

### When to Reconsider (Re-evaluation Triggers)

- **November 14, 2026 (FDA decision day):** This is the automatic trigger. Sell immediately after the FDA decision, regardless of outcome. Approval → reassess whether to hold for commercial ramp. Rejection → exit immediately (don't wait for the stock to "bounce").
- If an unexpected negative clinical data update drops before November 14, reassess the investment thesis immediately.

### Bear Case Reminder

The FDA rejects approximately 30-40% of drug applications in their first review. "Approval is likely" does not mean "approval is certain." This $100 could become $45 overnight on November 14. That's the risk you're accepting in exchange for the $200 potential.

## Risk Management Rules

**Rule 1: Do not add money beyond the $1,000 budget until the 6-month review.** Averaging down on losing positions is a classic beginner mistake. The research thesis may need to change, not the position size.

**Rule 2: SMMT is capped at $100 (10% of portfolio).** Do not add to this position regardless of how confident you feel before November 14. Binary events cut both ways.

**Rule 3: Do not sell on a bad week.** All five of these companies could drop 10-15% in a single week if the broader market sells off. That's normal. What matters is whether the underlying business is still growing. Only sell if a re-evaluation trigger fires, not because the stock went down.

**Rule 4: Check the portfolio no more than once per week.** Daily stock price checking is how good investments get turned into bad trades. These are 6-month to 2-year holds, not day trades.

**Rule 5: SMMT on November 14 — act immediately.** This is the one exception to Rule 3. The FDA decision is a binary outcome that immediately changes the thesis. Don't sit on it.
